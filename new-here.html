What to expect

Come as you are. Worship with us.

Everett Church of God is a welcoming Pentecostal church family with a contemporary worship environment. On Sunday, you can expect lively praise and worship and God's Word proclaimed with a desire to help people know Jesus and grow in faith.

Whether you've been in church for years or you're simply looking for answers, we want you to feel welcome here.
