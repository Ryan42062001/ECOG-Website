Our heart

Loving God. Loving People.

We want Everett Church of God to be a place where people can encounter the love of God, discover truth in Jesus Christ, find healing when they are hurting, and grow as part of a church family.

Our worship is contemporary and lively while remaining rooted in our faith in Jesus Christ. We believe God's Word should be proclaimed with the power and anointing of the Holy Spirit and lived out in the way we love and serve people.

What we believe

Rooted in God's Word and centered on Jesus.

The Bible

We believe that the Bible is God’s Word. It was written under divine inspiration. It contains all we need in order to know and walk with God.

God

We believe in one God who eternally exists in three persons. He is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, perfectly holy, sovereign, and good.

Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God. He is fully man and fully God, the one and only Lord and Savior, who died for our sins and was raised on the third day.

Salvation

We believe that salvation comes by grace through faith in Christ. It is a free gift for all who repent and believe; it’s impossible to earn, but it will completely transform our life and eternity.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the indwelling of the Holy Spirit. The Spirit of God seeks to fill us with His presence for the purpose of empowering His children to fulfill the great commission.

The Church

We believe in the Church. God designed His people to be gathered into churches who care for each other, grow more like Jesus, and make disciples among all nations.

Jesus Is Coming Back

We believe that Jesus is coming back. He will return to judge the living and the dead, condemning those who reject Him to perish, but rewarding His followers with eternal life.
