Our heart

Loving God. Loving People.

We want Everett Church of God to be a place where people can encounter the love of God, discover truth in Jesus Christ, find healing when they are hurting, and grow as part of a church family.

Our worship is contemporary and lively while remaining rooted in our faith in Jesus Christ. We believe God's Word should be proclaimed with the power and anointing of the Holy Spirit and lived out in the way we love and serve people.
